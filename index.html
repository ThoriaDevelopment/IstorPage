Privacy

Your research stays yours.

Istor runs entirely on your machine. That is a design decision, not a setting.

  • Local model

    Inference runs on your own hardware through open models. Nothing is sent to a server.

  • Local library

    Your sources live in a SQLite database on your own disk. Back it up, copy it, carry it.

  • No telemetry

    No accounts, no analytics, no usage reports. There is nothing to opt out of.

  • You trigger the traffic

    The only requests Istor makes are the fetches you ask for.

We have answers to your questions

Does Istor need an internet connection?

No. Research works fully offline. Istor only touches the network when you ask it to fetch a web source.

What hardware does it need?

A GTX 1650 class GPU runs the local roster comfortably. There is no cloud requirement, so the hardware you have is the hardware it uses.

Where is my data stored?

In a SQLite database on your own disk. The library can export a full backup as a zip at any time.

Can I choose which model to use?

Yes. The model roster is selectable in settings, and swapping models does not require a restart.

Who can see my sources?

Nobody. There is no account and no server. Your research is as private as your machine.
